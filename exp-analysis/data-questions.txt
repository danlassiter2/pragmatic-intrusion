More theoretically-driven questions:

      3. in the baseline no-QUD condition, do content types differ in terms of how false secondary content matters (a lot ~ conjunction, a little, not at all ~ ignored a la semantic-only judgments)?
        options: 
           a. truth-only: secondary content makes no difference to truth and probability judgments; should matter for acceptability judgments 
           b. no differences: primary and secondary content are treated as equivalent, so everything is like conjunction
                # hinted at by Kroll/Rysling result, but subtle for SI work + Cremers et al
          c. it's complicated: how the 2ndary dimension matters depends on content type. this is hinted at in previous work incl Kroll/Rysling and e.g. Beaver/Coppock on definiteness and more generally Tonhauser et al. 2010 on differences among presuppositions

      4. when we introduce QUD judgments: how does QUD influence effect of primary vs 2ndary content?
          a. QUD doesn't matter
          b. relevance dominant: QUD determines which dimension matters for judgment
          c. it's complicated: QUD should shift baseline results toward the emphasized dimension ...
                        (this is more or less what Kroll/Rysling found)
             but not deterministically, and in a way that depends on content type
